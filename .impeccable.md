## Design Context

### Users
The primary audience for the literature review generator is English-speaking coursework master’s students and final-year undergraduates. They need a structured first draft for a narrative literature review, often under a deadline, but must remain responsible for checking sources and adapting the work to their assignment requirements.

### Brand Personality
Calm, credible, encouraging. The tool should feel like a capable writing partner, not a shortcut that invites academic misconduct.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the established Story Generator visual system: warm off-white backgrounds, ink-toned text, amber primary actions, DM Sans body copy, and Source Serif display typography. The literature-review page may use paper, margin-note, and evidence-card motifs, but must not introduce a separate academic brand, a dark neon palette, or generic AI gradients.

### Design Principles
- Start with a clear academic task and guide only the necessary inputs.
- Make source limits and user responsibility visible before generation.
- Keep the anonymous first-use flow fast; reveal sign-in only when the existing quota rules require it.
- Preserve the existing responsive spacing, typography, color tokens, and accessibility behavior.
- Prefer calm evidence-oriented interfaces over decorative AI imagery.
